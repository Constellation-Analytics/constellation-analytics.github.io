About Me

Jump to the good stuff >

Originally from Australia, I've been living in Grenoble, France since 2023, where I work as a data analyst and analytics engineer.

I didn't exactly take the direct route into data. I started out as an automotive mechanic in 2009, diagnosing faults and solving problems with my hands. What I didn't realise at the time was that I was already doing data work, just with a set of wrenches instead of a keyboard. The root-cause analysis, the systems thinking, the satisfaction of finding why something wasn't working: that instinct never left me, it just found a better home.

After completing a Bachelor of Business in 2016, I moved through corporate roles spanning sales, supply chain, strategic planning, and recruitment. Each gave me a different lens on how organisations actually function and their common pain points. It was once I moved into data-focused roles that everything clicked; I realised I could apply that same mechanical curiosity to information systems, at scale, with far less back pain.

In 2022, I fully committed to pursuing a career in data and began a Master of Business Analytics, not to change direction, but to formalise and deepen skills I'd already been building in practice. That combination of hands-on experience and rigorous study shapes how I approach every problem: with business context, not just technical output.

Today I specialise in Power BI, SQL, Python, and the Microsoft Fabric ecosystem. I hold Microsoft certifications as an Excel Expert, Power BI Data Analyst Associate, Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate, and Fabric Data Engineer Associate.

What keeps me in this field is the same thing that kept me working on cars for years. There's always another problem to crack. Technology just gives you bigger, messier, more interesting problems. A tangled dataset that finally makes sense, a process that used to take all day now running in seconds. I don't think that feeling will ever get old.

Skills & Tools

My stack covers the full data lifecycle, from automated ingestion and cloud storage through to modelling and visualisation.

Data Visualisation & Reporting

Certified Microsoft Power BI Associate and Microsoft Certified Excel Expert. I design interactive dashboards and reports that translate complex datasets into clear, actionable insights for business stakeholders. Skilled in DAX, data modelling, and building reports with drill-through and dynamic filtering. Also experienced in Tableau and Excel, including advanced functions and Power Query for self-service reporting.

Data Pipelines & Automation

I build and maintain automated end-to-end data pipelines using Python. My workflows fetch data from web sources and external APIs, connect to cloud databases, run data quality checks, and execute stored procedures. I use GitHub for version control across all pipeline code, keeping everything reproducible, documented, and maintainable.

Cloud Infrastructure

My entire data stack runs in the cloud. I use Azure SQL and Neon for cloud-hosted database deployments, and GitHub Actions to schedule and trigger automated pipeline runs without manual intervention. Certified in Microsoft Fabric, with hands-on understanding of its unified analytics platform including OneLake, Lakehouses, and Warehouses.

Databases & Data Modelling

Proficient in SQL with a strong focus on PostgreSQL. I design normalised schemas, write optimised queries, and build stored procedures and views that form the foundation of reliable analytics workflows. Comfortable working across the full data modelling lifecycle, from raw ingestion layers through to clean, query-ready structures that feed directly into reporting and dashboards.
