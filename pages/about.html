About Me

Skip to the good stuff >

Originally from Australia, I've been living in Grenoble, France since 2023, where I work as a data analyst and analytics engineer.

I didn't exactly take the direct route into data. I started out as an automotive mechanic in 2009, diagnosing faults and solving problems with my hands. What I didn't realise at the time was that I was already doing data work, just with a set of wrenches instead of a keyboard. The root-cause analysis, the systems thinking, the satisfaction of finding why something wasn't working: that instinct never left me, it just found a better home.

After completing a Bachelor of Business in 2016, I moved through corporate roles spanning sales, supply chain, strategic planning, and recruitment. Each gave me a different lens on how organisations actually function and their common pain points. It was once I moved into data-focused roles that everything clicked; I realised I could apply that same mechanical curiosity to information systems, at scale, with far less back pain.

In 2022, I fully committed to pursuing a career in data and began a Master of Business Analytics, not to change direction, but to formalise and deepen skills I'd already been building in practice. That combination of hands-on experience and rigorous study shapes how I approach every problem: with business context, not just technical output.

Today I specialise in Power BI, SQL, Python, and the Microsoft Fabric ecosystem. I hold Microsoft certifications as an Excel Expert, Power BI Data Analyst Associate, Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate, and Fabric Data Engineer Associate.

But honestly, what keeps me in this field is simpler than any credential: I find data genuinely interesting. The puzzle of it. The moment a messy dataset starts telling a coherent story. I dont think that will ever get old.
